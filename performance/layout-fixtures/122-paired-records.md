# Portable evidence packages

Each package is an independent record. Its identifiers stay exact, and its purpose stays with the package instead of being squeezed between narrow columns.

## Package directory

| Service | Purpose | Artifact | Fingerprint | Repository | Steward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field archive | Retains original observations and supporting material for the reviewers who need to trace a decision back to its source. | `tachyon_archive_research_evidence_package_v1` | `research_evidence_fingerprint_reference_0001` | `research_archive_retained_materials_primary` | Research operations |
| Review bundle | Collects unresolved questions with their supporting context so the receiving team can continue the review without losing the original evidence. | `tachyon_review_delivery_evidence_package_v2` | `delivery_evidence_fingerprint_reference_0002` | `delivery_archive_retained_materials_primary` | Delivery team |
| Export record | Produces a portable snapshot for completed work, retaining the original observations without inventing missing conclusions. | `tachyon_export_completed_evidence_package_v3` | `completed_evidence_fingerprint_reference_003` | `completed_archive_retained_materials_primary` | |
