# Documents, thoughtfully composed

A calm editorial language for structured documents. Meaning chooses the form; measured content chooses the arrangement.

## Independent ideas, clearly arranged

Short features stay open. Shared typography and alignment provide their structure.

- **Markdown support:** Write in plain text with rich results.
- **A quieter workspace:** Keep attention on the document.
- **Adaptive layouts:** Let content find a comfortable shape.
- **Local ownership:** Keep your files on your own computer.
- **Clear relationships:** Preserve order, context, and meaning.
- **Reliable editing:** Select, revise, and undo in place.

## A sequence with a clear order

These short stages keep their authored numbers in every arrangement.

1. **Plan:** Clarify the goal and scope.
2. **Draft:** Give the ideas a first shape.
3. **Refine:** Improve the structure and flow.
4. **Review:** Check accuracy and clarity.
5. **Share:** Present the complete result.
6. **Learn:** Capture what the work revealed.

## A more considered reading rhythm

Good writing helps ideas travel. It gives complex concepts a clear shape, so people can understand, remember, and act on them. A document is more than a collection of boxes: it is a sequence of related thoughts, with deliberate moments of emphasis and enough room for the reader to follow the argument.

The visual language should support that journey. Headings mark a change in topic; paragraphs develop an idea; quotations preserve another voice. When several points share the same role, they can sit together. When an explanation needs sustained attention, a comfortable reading measure and a quiet page are often all it needs.

> Good documents make room for thought. Their structure helps the reader see what belongs together.
>
> — Editorial specimen
